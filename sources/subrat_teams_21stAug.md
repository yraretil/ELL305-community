> Some tips for the draft #1 of the Term Paper or Project paper on 24 Aug 2026
> Please note that the draft #1 is more in the nature of a progress report for work done so far rather than a preliminary version of the term paper.
>
> **For the TERM PAPER writers**
>
> What you are writing is
> Section 1 Work done as of 24 Aug 2026
> Use these points to write this section.
>
> 1. What is my chosen topic
> 2. How does it map to a Chapter in our textbook.
> I will consult Prof (Sumantra DuttaRoy | Subrat Kar} (choose one) as ADVISED
> 3. Show your progress as a Gantt chart (drawn in Latex) Suggested break-up Work-Package 1 (WP1)
>    * Task 1: Read the basic chapters # titled "" from the text by: ___ % done
>    * Task 2: Read papers related to Topic and store the references and PDFs in Zotero (share with Subrat Kar) WP2
>    * Task 3: Read ____ references so far (cite all of them)
> 4. ... ... ... (i have fuckall idea what this means)
>
> **FOR PROJECT students**
>
> Section 1 consists of inter alia:
>
> 1. What did you do so far?
> 2. Describe the experiments (0.5 page each along with one figure per experiment)
> 3. What is your plan
> 4. What is the design schedule
> 5. What is the procurement schedule ... .... ...
>
> In a report, codes deserve to be placed in an Appendix so they do not distract the reader (and save trees), with language specific color coded syntax.
